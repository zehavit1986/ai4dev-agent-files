# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete aiming to complete a marathon training day with carbohydrate loading:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3100 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (120g), Fat (85g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), honey (20g), and whole milk (240g). Two slices whole wheat toast (60g) with almond butter (20g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Pre-Training Snack (9:00 AM):** Energy bar (65g) with sports drink (500ml, 6% carbohydrate solution).
    * Protein: 8g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 85g

* **Lunch (12:30 PM):** Grilled chicken breast (150g), brown rice (120g cooked), steamed sweet potato (150g), and mixed vegetables (100g broccoli and carrots) with olive oil (15g).
    * Protein: 42g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 95g

* **Afternoon Snack (3:30 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g, full-fat) with granola (40g) and blueberries (80g). Bagel (70g) with jam (25g).
    * Protein: 20g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Baked salmon (130g), pasta with tomato sauce (150g cooked pasta, 100g sauce), side salad with olive oil dressing (30g oil).
    * Protein: 32g, Fat: 22g, Carbs: 60g

* **Evening Snack (9:30 PM):** Chocolate milk (240ml) with a peanut butter sandwich on white bread (40g peanut butter, 50g bread).
    * Protein: 15g, Fat: 13g, Carbs: 45g

This meal plan emphasizes complex and simple carbohydrates to maximize glycogen storage, includes adequate protein for muscle recovery, and provides sustained energy throughout the marathon training day.
