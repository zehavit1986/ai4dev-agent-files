# One-Day Meal Plan for Elite Marathon Runner

Here is a one-day meal plan optimized for an elite marathon runner preparing for a 20-mile training run with strategic carbohydrate and recovery nutrition timing:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (130g), Fat (85g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with honey (30g), banana (120g), and whole milk (240ml). Side of whole wheat toast (60g) with almond butter (20g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Pre-Run Snack (8:00 AM - 2 hours before run):** Sports drink (500ml) with electrolytes and white bread (80g) with jam (25g).
    * Protein: 2g, Fat: 1g, Carbs: 65g

* **During 20-Mile Run (10:00 AM):** Sports drink with carbohydrates (750ml total) and energy gels (2 x 30g packets, consumed every 45 minutes).
    * Protein: 0g, Fat: 0g, Carbs: 80g

* **Post-Run Recovery Meal (1:00 PM - within 30 minutes of finishing):** Chocolate milk (500ml), chicken breast (150g), white rice (150g cooked), and steamed carrots (100g).
    * Protein: 50g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 95g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g) with granola (50g) and berries (80g).
    * Protein: 20g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 50g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Lean ground turkey (160g), whole wheat pasta (120g cooked), marinara sauce (150g), and side salad with olive oil dressing (30ml).
    * Protein: 35g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 65g

* **Evening Recovery Snack (9:30 PM):** Cottage cheese (150g) with pineapple (100g) and almonds (15g).
    * Protein: 28g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 20g

This meal plan strategically times carbohydrates before, during, and after the 20-mile training run to maximize endurance performance and accelerate recovery, while providing adequate protein for muscle repair and adaptation.
